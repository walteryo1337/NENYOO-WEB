About Nenyoo

Mod menus for GTA V, FiveM and RDR2, alongside public creator resources, setup guides and a shared account service.

Explore first. Choose what fits.

This website brings together the Nenyoo store, shared-loader downloads, community presets and documentation. GTA V has free Essential access plus Standard and Premium editions. FiveM has a separate product and a trial of the free features; RDR2 has its own product. RedM and Rainbow Six Siege are listed as coming soon.

The public resources are open to browse before you create an account. Use the interactive menu preview, explore vehicle and outfit presets, or try the developer tools to see what is useful for your projects.

Good to know

Can I use my existing account?

Yes. Sign in with your existing Nenyoo credentials or Discord. Public product information, guides, tools and preset catalogs can be browsed before signing in.

Where are my purchases?

Payment and receipts are handled by the card or crypto checkout provider. Redeem your purchased key here to see the license on your dashboard.

When can I reset my HWID?

Check My Products in your dashboard for reset availability and any cooldown. If a reset is unavailable or you need help, contact support.

How does chat work?

Sign in and open the dashboard to join the community chat. Access depends on your membership.

How do I change my profile picture?

Open Profile & Discord and save a public HTTPS image URL.
