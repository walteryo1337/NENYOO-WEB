Find and protect your paid key

Find your license email, keep proof of purchase, and get help when a key has not arrived.

Updated September 23, 2026

Find the key email

  1. 1
    Check the email address used at checkout

    For Shopify purchases, the order confirmation and license-key email are separate messages. The confirmation contains your invoice; the later message contains the key.

  2. 2
    Check spam and junk

    Search your inbox and spam folders for Nenyoo, the order confirmation, and the license-key message. Mark the key email as not spam and save a separate backup.

  3. 3
    Keep the complete key and purchase confirmation

    Save the full key, order number, and receipt somewhere private. Support may need these to investigate a purchase issue. Do not publish them in a public Discord channel.

  4. 4
    Redeem on the website

    Sign in and open My Products / Redeem a key. If you need an account, follow Create your account.

The key has not arrived

If you have a purchase confirmation but cannot find the key email, open a private support ticket through Support. Include your order number, purchase date, store or reseller, and receipt. Describe what you received and which inbox folders you checked. Never provide your password.

Older purchases and lost keys

Old Sellix, SellApp, or Sellpass links may no longer contain your order or key. Do not rely on an old store link as your only backup. The Discord key guide states that lost keys cannot be recovered or replaced, so keep your own secure copy of your key and login details.
