Launch and inject in GTA V

GTA V Legacy and Enhanced setup, including BattlEye enabled or disabled instructions for each Nenyoo tier.

Updated September 23, 2026

The steps are the same for GTA V Legacy and Enhanced. The only difference between tiers is BattlEye. Pick your tier first:

  • Disable BattlEye before launching.
  • You can play alone or with other modders. Standard users must be the session host to play with others; joining a session hosted by another player will result in being kicked. See BattlEye and sessions.

Inject

  1. 1
    Open the loader and choose your product

    Sign in and select the product you own.

  2. 2
    Start GTA V

    Click Launch Game in the loader, or start the game yourself. Either works.

  3. 3
    Wait for the landing page

    Stop at the screen where you choose Story Mode or Online. Don’t pick either yet.

  4. 4
    Click Load

    Wait until a console window opens.

  5. 5
    Enter Story Mode

    The menu’s open key appears on screen once it’s ready. See Menu controls.

  6. 6
    Join a session from Story Mode

    Essential/Standard: join an Invite Only session. Premium: join any session. If joining through the menu fails, use the GTA V pause menu instead.

ImportantDon’t go straight into Online

Injecting after you’ve chosen a mode, or loading directly into Online, makes the game crash or return to the main menu, or shows “BattlEye is required to play GTA Online”.

Heads upEnhanced: turn off Frame Generation

In GTA V Enhanced, open Settings → Graphics and turn Frame Generation off. Leaving it on causes a black screen or crash.
