BattlEye and sessions

BattlEye enabled and disabled sessions in GTA Online: Nenyoo tier requirements, session compatibility, and desync troubleshooting.

Updated September 23, 2026

  • BattlEye on (Premium): public sessions with regular players.
  • BattlEye off (Essential): solo, or with other players who also have BattlEye off.
  • BattlEye off (Standard): you can join public sessions, but you will be kicked or desynced when another player is the session host. You can stay in the session when you are the host.

With Standard, joining a public session does not guarantee you can stay. When another player is the session host, you can be kicked or desynced. To stay in a public session, you must be the session host. If host ownership changes to another player, you can be kicked or desynced again.

Hosting with BattlEye disabled

Hosting prevents Standard from being removed by another session host, but it does not make every player’s connection stable. Players with BattlEye enabled may still be desynced from your session. Friends using a compatible BattlEye-disabled setup can play together; an Invite Only session is the simpler option for a group.

If you are not the host, a kick after a few minutes can be the expected heartbeat / session compatibility behavior rather than an injection failure. FSL does not fix this: it redirects save data to disk and is not a session-compatibility bypass.

Does FSL let me play public sessions?

No. FSL converts your online save into a local save; that’s all it does. It won’t stop desync kicks. We can’t support problems caused by FSL.

I removed FSL and my progress disappeared.

FSL stores progress on disk, so that progress only shows while FSL is installed.

I removed FSL and the game won’t start.

Delete Documents\GTAV Enhanced\Profiles and start the game again.

Can Standard stay in a public session?

Yes, while you are the session host. If another player is the host, you will be kicked or desynced.

Heads up

Modding always carries a ban risk. If you can’t accept that risk, don’t mod on an account you care about.
