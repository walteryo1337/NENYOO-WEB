Getting started

How to use Nenyoo for FiveM

Choose the dedicated FiveM product, set up your account and loader, and follow the video for the launch flow.

Updated November 13, 2026

FiveM uses a dedicated Nenyoo product. A GTA V Essential, Standard or Premium key is not the FiveM offer. Review the FiveM product page before choosing access.

Before you launch

  1. 1
    Choose FiveM access

    The store lists a paid FiveM build and a four-hour trial of the free features. Use the free-key link on the FiveM page for that route; check its current instructions before continuing.

  2. 2
    Prepare your account

    Follow Create your account to register, verify your email and redeem a purchased key. Keep your purchase email and receipt.

  3. 3
    Install the shared loader

    Open Downloads and follow Install the loader for Windows preparation and runtimes. Sign in and select the FiveM product.

  4. 4
    Check the current requirements

    Read Compatibility & requirements. Confirm your current FiveM build and any server restrictions with support; the website does not publish a tested build list.

Video walkthrough

The video below is the existing FiveM walkthrough. Use it alongside the current product page and loader prompts; interface details can change between builds.

If the loader or game does not start

Start with Loader won’t open. If the issue continues, record the exact error, FiveM build and Nenyoo version, then follow Collect logs and report a bug. Do not follow GTA V BattlEye instructions as if they were a FiveM setup guide.
