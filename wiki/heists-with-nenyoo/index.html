Using the menu

Heists with Nenyoo

Check edition and session requirements, use the heist video walkthrough, and find the right help for missing options or errors.

Updated November 13, 2026

The GTA V comparison table lists Heist Editor for Standard and Premium. Check the current edition comparison before following a walkthrough, since options depend on your product and installed build.

Prepare before following the walkthrough

  • Follow Launch and inject in GTA V for your edition and tier before starting a heist.
  • Read BattlEye and sessions for the documented session and host requirements.
  • Use Menu controls if you need help opening the menu or navigating submenus.
  • The interactive menu preview lets you explore the menu structure in your browser. It does not run game actions or establish compatibility with your installed build.

Video walkthrough

Use the existing video below as the walkthrough. This page does not promise specific payouts, persistence or a tested result for the current game update.

Missing options or unexpected results

Check the product and version you loaded first. If an option differs from the video, include its exact menu label, your GTA V edition/build, Nenyoo tier and what happened in a support report. Use Compatibility & requirements to see what information needs confirmation.
