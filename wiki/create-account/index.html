Getting started

Create your account

Sign up on the website, verify your email, and redeem your license key.

Updated September 23, 2026

Create and manage your Nenyoo account on this website. You do not need the loader to register or redeem a key.

Create an account and sign in

  1. 1
    Open the sign-up page

    Go to Create an account. Enter your username, email, and password, confirm your password, and accept the terms to register.

  2. 2
    Verify your email

    Enter the verification code sent to your email. Check spam or junk if it does not arrive, or use Resend code on the verification screen.

  3. 3
    Sign in

    Open Log in and sign in with your username and password. Already have an account? Skip registration and sign in with your existing account.

Need your purchase email? See Find and protect your paid key.

Redeem your license key

  1. 1
    Open your products page

    Go to My Products / Redeem a key while signed in.

  2. 2
    Enter your full key

    Under Have a license key?, paste the complete license key from your purchase email into Enter your activation key. Check spam or junk if you cannot find the email.

  3. 3
    Activate your product

    Click Activate product, then check your licenses on the same page.

TipKeep your account details safe

Save your license key and purchase confirmation somewhere safe. If you forget your password, use Reset password. For activation problems, contact Support.

Once your product is active, open Downloads and follow Install the loader when you are ready to play.
